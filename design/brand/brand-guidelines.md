> Scope: Brand identity, naming, wordmark, logo direction, iconography, brand voice, usage rules, digital applications and 3D environment integration

---

# 1. Brand Purpose

AquaGuard is a smart water-management and monitoring platform that connects:

- physical water infrastructure
- sensors
- automation
- real-time monitoring
- cloud data
- analytics
- human control

The brand must communicate this relationship clearly.

AquaGuard is not positioned as:

- a generic water app
- a simple tank-level indicator
- a basic Arduino project
- a generic IoT dashboard

The identity should communicate a complete intelligent system.
