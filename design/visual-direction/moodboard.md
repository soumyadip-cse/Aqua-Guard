## 1. Design Vision

AquaGuard is an intelligent water-management and monitoring system presented through an immersive digital control environment.

The visual experience must combine five qualities:

1. Engineering precision
2. Water and fluidity
3. Advanced technology
4. Calmness and trust
5. Spatial depth through 3D

The interface should feel like a **modern engineering control room**, not a generic IoT dashboard.

The experience must communicate:

```text
Observe
→ Understand
→ Detect
→ Decide
→ Control
→ Record
→ Analyze
